# Evidence of Relevance

## Social & Environmental Impact
Frequent catastrophic flood events cause significant infrastructure damage, human displacement, and economic loss worldwide. Rapid, automated flood extent mapping is essential for disaster response agencies, local governance, and emergency services.

## Technical Relevance of SAR Data
- **All-Weather Capability:** Unlike optical satellites (e.g., Sentinel-2, Landsat), Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) sensors like Sentinel-1 penetrate heavy cloud cover, storm conditions, and operate during nighttime.
- **Automated Rapid Response:** Automated processing pipelines remove the bottleneck of manual GIS analysis, providing decision-makers with inundation footprints within minutes of satellite pass-over.
