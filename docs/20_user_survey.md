# User Needs and Usability Survey

## Purpose

This short survey gathers feedback on the needs of people who may use a flood mapping and settlement isolation platform. The target respondents are students and researchers familiar with GIS, remote sensing, flood risk, or emergency response. It does not ask respondents to claim experience they do not have, and it does not collect names or email addresses.

**Target:** 20 genuine respondents. Treat this as exploratory feedback, not a representative sample. Report the actual number of completed responses and do not create or fill responses on behalf of other people.

**Estimated time:** 3–5 minutes.

## Form title and description

**Title:** Flood Mapping and Settlement Isolation: User Needs Survey

**Description:** We are evaluating a research prototype that uses satellite radar and road network data to map potential flood extent and identify settlements that may be cut off from road access. This survey asks about your role and what information would be useful. Participation is voluntary. Do not include names, contact details, or sensitive operational information. You may skip any question. Responses will be summarized for an academic project; individual responses will not be identified. The prototype is for research and is not certified for emergency operations.

**Consent question (required, multiple choice):** I have read the description and agree to take part in this anonymous survey.

- Yes, I agree
- No, I do not agree *(set form branching to submit/exit)*

## Questions

1. **Which option best describes your current role?** *(Required, multiple choice)*
   - Emergency response or civil protection
   - Local or regional government / public administration
   - GIS, remote sensing, or mapping
   - Disaster risk, humanitarian aid, or infrastructure planning
   - Student or academic researcher
   - Other
   - Prefer not to say

2. **How familiar are you with flood response, flood mapping, or disaster planning?** *(Required, linear scale 1–5)*
   - 1 — Not familiar
   - 5 — Very familiar

3. **Have you used maps or digital tools to assess flood impacts or access to settlements?** *(Required, multiple choice)*
   - Yes, regularly
   - Yes, occasionally
   - No, but it is relevant to my work or studies
   - No

4. **When assessing a flood, how useful would each information type be to you?** *(Required, multiple-choice grid; rows: flood extent; affected roads/bridges; settlements potentially cut off; date/time of satellite observation; confidence or limitations of the result. Columns: Not useful, Slightly useful, Moderately useful, Very useful, Essential, Unsure)*

5. **Which outputs would be most useful in a flood mapping tool? Choose up to 3.** *(Checkboxes; response validation maximum 3)*
   - Map of likely flooded areas
   - List/map of potentially isolated settlements
   - Affected road segments and crossings
   - Comparison between dates
   - Downloadable map or report
   - Explanation of uncertainty and data limitations
   - Other

6. **How important is it to know when the satellite image was captured and how current the map is?** *(Required, linear scale 1–5)*
   - 1 — Not important
   - 5 — Essential

7. **What would make you hesitate to rely on a satellite-based flood or isolation map?** *(Optional, checkboxes)*
   - Results may be out of date
   - Water detection may be inaccurate
   - Roads or settlements may be missing from the map data
   - The map may not explain uncertainty
   - I would need training to interpret the result
   - I would need to verify it with local reports
   - Other

8. **How easy would it be for you to interpret a map that marks settlements as potentially isolated when their mapped road connections cross detected flood areas?** *(Required, linear scale 1–5)*
   - 1 — Very difficult
   - 5 — Very easy
   - Add “I would need to see an example” as an option if using multiple choice instead.

9. **What would you want the tool to explain before acting on an isolation result?** *(Optional, paragraph)*

10. **What one change would make this kind of tool more useful for your work or studies?** *(Optional, paragraph)*
